# Delete a TripFit Collaboration Account

## Delete in the App

1. Open TripFit and select the **More** tab.
2. Select **Collaboration Account**.
3. Select **Delete Collaboration Account**.
4. Review the notice and select **Delete Firebase Account Only**.
5. Complete Sign in with Apple reauthentication if requested.
6. Confirm the **Collaboration Account Deleted** message.

Deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account, Shared Trip membership data, and Shared Trips owned by the user. Private wardrobe, outfit, private trip, and iCloud data are not deleted automatically. An Apple subscription must also be canceled separately.

If you cannot complete deletion in the App, open a [support issue](https://github.com/bbdyno/TripFit/issues/new). Do not include your Apple Account details or invitation links in a public issue.
