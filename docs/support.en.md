# TripFit English Support

## Quick Troubleshooting

1. Update TripFit to the latest version.
2. Fully close and reopen the App.
3. For Shared Trips, check your network and sign-in status under **More → Collaboration Account**.
4. For iCloud sync, check your iCloud sign-in, available storage, and TripFit sync settings.

## Data and Account

- Wardrobe, outfits, and private trips are stored separately from your collaboration account.
- Delete a collaboration account: [step-by-step guide](../account-deletion.en/)
- Privacy details: [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy.en/)
- Manage subscriptions: iPhone **Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions**

## Contact

Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/bbdyno/TripFit/issues/new) with the app version, iOS version, reproduction steps, and screenshots. Do not post invitation links, Apple Account details, or private trip content in a public issue.
